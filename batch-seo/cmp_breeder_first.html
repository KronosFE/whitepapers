Breeder-first vs single-machine fusion.

Most fusion companies chase net electricity from one machine. Kronos splits the problem: breed the fuel and strategic materials first, then burn the fuel for power.

Side by side

One hard bet vs a staged program.

Single machine
One machine, one goal
  • Everything rides on net electricity from a single device
  • No product until the last, hardest step works
  • A single point of failure for the whole thesis
Kronos
Two machines, staged
  • Breeder makes helium-3, tritium, 14 MeV neutrons — a product before a plant
  • The physics that must close is solved at demonstrated confinement (Q 3.424)
  • Burner is optimized purely to convert fuel to power
  • Value at each step, not only at the end

Fuel follows purpose, not platform: breed what's needed, burn it where it's needed.
