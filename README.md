# Kronos Fusion Energy — Whitepaper Library

104 short design-and-physics whitepapers on the Kronos compact fusion platform: the **HYPERION**
spherical-tokamak D–T breeder and the **D–³He tandem-mirror generator** (housed as **AEGIS** and
**MetroVolt**). Each paper is numbered `KFE-WP001…KFE-WP104`, grouped into seven series, and traces
every figure to the openly deposited Kronos simulation programme and design-point records.

**Live:** [kronosfusionenergy.com/whitepapers](https://www.kronosfusionenergy.com/whitepapers)

## Structure
- `index.html` — library index (all papers, by series)
- `KFE-WP0NN_<slug>.html` — one page per whitepaper
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt` — search-engine + AI-crawler discovery
- `build_whitepapers.py` — static generator; regenerate with `python3 build_whitepapers.py`

## Series
Hyperion — The Breeder · The Burner (Aegis & MetroVolt) · Low-Neutron by Design · Direct Energy
Conversion & Power Handling · The Fuel Cycle (Tritium & Helium-3) · Method, Safety & Trust ·
The Mode D2/M Evolution.

## Notes
Conceptual design and simulation study — **no machine has been built.** Quantitative values are
simulation-derived and carry the feasibility gates named in the text; superseded values are kept in
the record with era labels. The formal papers and reproducible data are on the
[Publications page](https://www.kronosfusionenergy.com/publications) and the
[Zenodo community](https://zenodo.org/communities/kronos_fusion_energy) (Open Access, CC BY 4.0).
