The Burner's Reference Point: 197 MW at Q_E 0.63, Unchannelled

Before any speculative physics, the mirror is a fully specified 197 MW design point.

The burner's reference point is a 197 MW, 128 m³ D-3He mirror at engineering gain Q_E = 0.632, computed with no alpha channelling assumed. It is a real, self-consistent design point — and it does not yet reach net electricity, which the record states plainly.

The science

The reference solve computes every channel: fusion power by cross section, radiation by synchrotron and bremsstrahlung, axial loss by ambipolar theory, and confinement from Pastukhov theory. It reproduces the prior evaluator's headline Q_E from independent physics (0.632 vs 0.657, 3.8% apart) while correcting three of its supporting numbers.

Why it matters

Publishing a below-unity baseline is the honesty that makes the rest credible. The burner is 'an experiment, not a power plant' at this point, and the single parameter that would move it to closure is named rather than hidden.
